# Salesforce Environment Assessment

## Purpose
Evaluate the current Salesforce platform foundation and its readiness to support enterprise AI workloads.

## Questions

### Org Structure & Editions
1. What Salesforce edition is in use? (Enterprise / Unlimited / Platform / Other)
2. Is Salesforce Data Cloud licensed and activated? (Yes/No/Evaluating)
3. How many Salesforce orgs are in your environment? (1 / 2-5 / 5+)
4. Are sandboxes used for development and testing? (Yes/No/Partial)
5. Is a DevOps process in place for Salesforce deployments? (1-5 scale)

### Data Cloud Configuration
6. Is Data Cloud connected to external data sources? (Yes/No)
7. Are data streams configured for real-time ingestion? (Yes/No)
8. Is identity resolution configured for customer matching? (Yes/No)
9. Are segments defined and activated? (Yes/No)
10. Is Data Cloud connected to Marketing Cloud or other activation targets? (Yes/No)

### Integration
11. Is MuleSoft deployed for integration? (Yes/No)
12. How many external systems are integrated with Salesforce? (1-5 / 6-20 / 20+)
13. Are connected apps configured with OAuth 2.0? (Yes/No)
14. Is Change Data Capture (CDC) enabled for key objects? (Yes/No)

### AppExchange & Customization
15. How many AppExchange packages are installed? (0-5 / 6-20 / 20+)
16. What is the level of custom Apex/LWC development? (1-5 scale)
