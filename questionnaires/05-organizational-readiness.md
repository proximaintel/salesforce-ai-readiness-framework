# Organizational Readiness Assessment

## Purpose
Evaluate the organizational capacity, skills, and culture to adopt and sustain AI on Salesforce.

## Questions

### Leadership & Strategy
1. Is there C-level sponsorship for AI on Salesforce? (Yes/No)
2. Is AI part of the Salesforce platform strategy? (Yes/No)
3. Is there a defined AI strategy with measurable outcomes? (1-5 scale)
4. Is there a dedicated budget for AI beyond existing Salesforce licensing? (Yes/No)

### Skills & Talent
5. How many staff have Salesforce AI certifications (Einstein, Data Cloud)? (0 / 1-3 / 4-10 / 10+)
6. Are Salesforce admins trained on Einstein AI features? (Yes/No)
7. Is there a training plan for Data Cloud and Agentforce? (Yes/No)
8. Are business users trained on AI literacy (what AI can/cannot do)? (1-5 scale)

### Change Management
9. Is there a change management process for AI feature rollouts? (Yes/No)
10. Are end users engaged in AI use case prioritization? (1-5 scale)
11. Is there a process for measuring AI business outcomes (adoption, ROI)? (Yes/No)

### Operations & Support
12. Is there a defined operating model for AI features on Salesforce? (Yes/No/Partial)
13. Are SLAs defined for AI model performance? (Yes/No)
14. Is there a process for handling AI errors or incorrect predictions? (Yes/No)
15. Are Salesforce partners or consultants engaged for AI delivery? (Yes/No)

## Scoring Weight
This section contributes **10%** to the overall AI Readiness Score.
